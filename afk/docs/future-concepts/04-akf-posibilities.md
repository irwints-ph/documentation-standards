I love this direction because it shifts the focus from **what AFK is** to **what AFK enables**.

What you've listed are exactly those kinds of problems.

# AFK — Example Applications (Draft)

Instead of introducing AFK through theory, introduce it through journeys.

> "Here are real problems that AFK can help solve."

---

## 1. Learning & Education

### Objective

Acquire and retain knowledge efficiently.

Examples:

* Refresh arithmetic through calculus
* Learn physics from first principles
* Understand electronics
* Learn programming
* Study database design
* Learn system architecture
* Learn AI fundamentals

Example project:

> **Designing and Building a Dog House**

Instead of simply teaching mathematics, AFK would answer:

* Why do I need geometry?
* How do I calculate roof angles?
* How much lumber should I buy?
* How much paint is required?
* How do I estimate the total cost?
* How do I optimize material usage?

Knowledge is immediately connected to practical application.

# Future Vision

This is where I think AFK begins to differentiate itself.

Many AI workflows aim to generate answers.

AFK aims to generate **capability**.

The desired outcome is not:

> "I completed a conversation."

The desired outcome is:

> "I can now do something I couldn't do before."

---

## One idea I would add

Eventually, I would introduce a document called something like:

> **AFK Use Cases**

Rather than listing features, it would tell stories.

* "How AFK helped document a legacy React application."
* "How AFK guided the design of a Bluetooth amplifier accessory."
* "How AFK supported preparation for a master's entrance exam."
* "How AFK turned a song idea into a finished composition."
* "How AFK helped someone relearn mathematics by applying it to building a dog house."

Those stories would demonstrate that AFK is not tied to engineering. Engineering is simply the first domain where the methodology is being validated. Once the process is proven there, the same approach can be adapted to many other kinds of learning and creation.
