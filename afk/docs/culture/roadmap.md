### Emerging Themes

AFK is gradually converging around several foundational values:

- Evidence over opinion
- Curiosity before certainty
- Progressive methodology
- Engineering humility
- Continuous learning
- Human-first collaboration
- Decision traceability
- Engineering freedom

These themes have emerged through real engineering collaboration and will become standalone culture documents only after they have been repeatedly validated across multiple projects.

AFK intentionally avoids planning culture in advance.

Culture is discovered through practice.

Future culture documents should emerge naturally rather than being planned.
