# 🌱 Progressive Methodology

> Engineering process should emerge from engineering need.

---

# Purpose

AFK intentionally begins with the smallest process capable of solving the current engineering problem.

Rather than introducing every possible artifact from the beginning, AFK allows methodology to grow together with project complexity.

---

# Philosophy

Engineering process is not the objective.

Engineering understanding is.

The methodology exists only to support engineering understanding.

---

# Principle

> Let the problem pull the process.

Do not let the process dictate the work.

---

# Default Assumptions

For most projects the initial stakeholders are simply:

- Human Collaborator
- AI Collaborator

No additional stakeholder management is required.

---

# As Complexity Grows

AFK progressively introduces:

- Additional stakeholders
- Evidence sources
- Decision authority
- Governance
- Validation
- Operational continuity

Only when necessary.

# Benefits

- Minimal overhead
- Easier adoption
- Naturally scalable
- Less bureaucracy
- Better engineering focus

---

# Engineering Note

AFK does not attempt to predict every document that may be needed.

Instead, AFK allows documentation and process to emerge as engineering discoveries reveal genuine needs.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Simplicity first.

> Complexity only when justified.

Engineering should remain lightweight until reality proves otherwise.
