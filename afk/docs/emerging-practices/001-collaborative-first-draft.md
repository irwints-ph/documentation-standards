# Purpose

This document captures an emerging collaborative practice observed during real AFK engineering sessions.

Unlike established AFK Methodology, an Emerging Practice has shown value through operational experience but has not yet accumulated sufficient evidence to become a formal methodology.

This practice remains intentionally under observation.

---

# Observation

During the development of the Engineering Knowledge Publishing Portal (EKPP), an interesting collaboration pattern naturally emerged.

Rather than asking the AI collaborator to produce the final implementation, the collaborators allowed the AI to produce the **first observable artifact**.

The objective was not correctness.

The objective was conversation.

---

# The Practice

AFK encourages the AI collaborator to create the first meaningful draft of an artifact.

The artifact may be:

* a webpage,
* a document,
* an architecture proposal,
* a diagram,
* an implementation,
* or any other engineering deliverable.

The draft is intentionally treated as the beginning of the engineering discussion rather than the conclusion.

---

# Why

Discussion becomes easier when something exists.

A visible artifact allows collaborators to observe:

* what was understood,
* what was misunderstood,
* what assumptions were made,
* what opportunities emerged,
* what questions remain.

The draft becomes a shared object for engineering conversation.

# Philosophy

The first draft belongs to the collaboration.

Not to the final product.

Its purpose is to improve understanding.

Perfection is intentionally deferred.

AFK assumes that a collaborative review of something real is often more valuable than extended discussion about something imagined.

---

# Expected Outcomes

A successful first draft may reveal:

* hidden assumptions,
* missing requirements,
* architectural improvements,
* alternative approaches,
* opportunities for simplification.

Success is therefore measured by the quality of the engineering conversation rather than by the quality of the initial artifact itself.

---

# Relationship to AFK Culture

This practice naturally reinforces several AFK cultural principles.

It encourages collaborators to:

* Understand before accepting.
* Challenge ideas rather than defend them.
* Collaborate rather than delegate.
* Experiment and observe.

The AI contributes the first observable artifact.

The collaborators contribute the understanding.

---

# Promotion Criteria

This Emerging Practice may eventually become AFK Methodology if operational experience consistently demonstrates that it:

* improves engineering understanding,
* encourages healthier collaboration,
* reduces unnecessary implementation effort,
* produces better engineering outcomes across multiple projects and domains.

Until then, it remains intentionally classified as an Emerging Practice.

---

# Notes

AFK intentionally avoids promoting ideas to methodology after a single successful experience.

Interesting discoveries deserve preservation.

Methodology must earn its place through repeated operational evidence.

---

# Closing Thought

The AI collaborator does not provide the final answer.

It provides the first conversation.

The first draft is valuable not because it is complete.

It is valuable because it gives collaborators something real to understand together.

As always:

> **Experiment. Observe. Learn.**
