# 🎵 The AFK Song (Draft)
Lyrics by Erwin T. Santos & ChatGPT
---

## Verse 1

It started with a quiet sigh,

"I'm writing this again... but why?"

Another project, another page,

The same old notes, another stage.

Then came a thought that wouldn't fade,

"Can this be easier someday?"

---

## Verse 2

Months went by, I came back home,

To half-built dreams and code unknown.

"What did I know?"

"Where did I start?"

The answers weren't inside my heart.

So I asked again,

"How do I remember?"

---

## Verse 3

Then AI knocked upon my door,

It answered once... then asked for more.

Every session, every day,

I kept explaining yesterday.

Until one question changed it all...

"How do I preserve engineering state?"

## Chorus

**AFK.**

We just...

**Naturally AFK'd it.**

For every teammate,

For every AI...

That one day would ask...

"Where do we begin?"

AFK would answer.
