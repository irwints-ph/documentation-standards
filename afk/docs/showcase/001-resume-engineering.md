# Purpose

Assess the current resume as an engineering artifact.

The objective is not to rewrite the resume immediately.

The objective is to understand:

- what evidence it presents,
- what evidence is missing,
- how it aligns with the current market,
- and what improvements will most increase the probability of achieving the Current Engineering Wish.

This document serves as the engineering baseline for future resume iterations.
