# 🤝 Engineering Handoff Template

---

# Purpose

This document concludes **Journey 1 — Discovery**.

Its purpose is to transfer the engineering understanding developed during discovery to the next engineering journey.

Rather than documenting implementation details, this document summarizes:

* repository understanding
* engineering confidence
* repository readiness
* important discoveries
* remaining unknowns
* recommended next steps

This document serves as the official engineering handoff between AFK journeys.
