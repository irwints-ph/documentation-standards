## AFK

AFK (Assisted Flow of Knowledge) is emerging as an AI-assisted engineering learning methodology.

Current understanding:

> AFK is not a documentation methodology.

It is a methodology for helping engineers understand systems, preserve knowledge, and continuously improve through structured AI-assisted collaboration.

Current concepts include:

* AI-Assisted Collaboration
* AI-Assisted Learning
* Engineering Session Continuity
* Knowledge Packages
* WWAN (Where We Are Now)
* Discovery Work Packages
* Canon Events
* Sustainable Engineering

# Major Discoveries This Session

Several important concepts emerged.

## 1. AI-Assisted Collaboration

The heart of AFK is no longer documentation.

It is AI-assisted collaboration that improves engineering understanding while preserving human responsibility for validation and decision-making.

---

## 2. Documentation Is an Artifact

A recurring principle emerged:

```text
Understanding

↓

Documentation
```

Documentation is no longer viewed as the objective.

It is an artifact produced by understanding.

---

## 3. Sustainable Engineering

A new research direction emerged.

Rather than asking:

> "How do we make AI greener?"

AFK asks:

> "How do we design engineering systems that naturally waste less?"

The current hypothesis:

> The greenest computation is the computation you never have to repeat.

Knowledge preservation reduces repeated engineering effort and repeated AI usage.

---

## 4. WWAN

The "Where We Are Now" document has become a first-class AFK artifact.

Purpose:

* Preserve current understanding
* Support session continuity
* Enable rapid onboarding
* Reduce repeated context reconstruction

Nickname:

> **Wow One**

Read one document.

Understand where the project stands.

# Session Reflection

One observation became increasingly clear during this session:

We are no longer simply documenting software.

We are documenting **how engineers learn software**.

That shift—from documenting systems to documenting the process of understanding systems—marks the emerging identity of AFK and the broader Engineering Knowledge System.

---

I think this captures where we genuinely are.

A week ago, we were building a documentation framework.

Today, we're building something broader: a methodology for **AI-assisted engineering learning** that just happens to produce excellent documentation as one of its outcomes.

And I have to say, I really like the symbolism of ending the day by updating the **WWAN**. It already feels like a natural AFK ritual:

> **Before you stop building, preserve today's understanding for tomorrow's engineer.**
