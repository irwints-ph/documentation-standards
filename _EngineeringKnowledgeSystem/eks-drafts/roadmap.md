# AFK Roadmap

---

## Vision

Build a practical AI-assisted knowledge system that helps people transform curiosity into usable knowledge.

The roadmap intentionally evolves from real-world discovery rather than speculative design.

# Guiding Principles

The roadmap follows several principles.

- Curiosity comes first.
- Evidence before standards.
- Learn by building.
- Preserve reasoning.
- Small discoveries accumulate.
- Human judgment remains essential.
- AI accelerates, humans validate.

---

# Success Metric

Success is not measured by the number of documents created.

Success is measured by whether someone can begin with curiosity...

...and finish with usable knowledge.
