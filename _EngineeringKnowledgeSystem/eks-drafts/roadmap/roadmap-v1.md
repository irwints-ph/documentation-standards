# Success Criteria

The Engineering Knowledge System is successful when an engineer can:

- Understand an unknown system.
- Validate findings.
- Produce consistent documentation.
- Discover architecture.
- Derive standards.
- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Transfer knowledge with minimal dependency on individuals.
