# Purpose

This document captures the wishes that currently guide the evolution of the Engineering Knowledge Publishing Portal (EKPP).

Unlike traditional requirements, wishes intentionally describe the desired outcome rather than a technical solution.

The objective is to understand **what** we are trying to achieve before deciding **how** it should be implemented.

This document serves as an input to architecture rather than an implementation checklist.

---

# Guiding Principle

AFK encourages beginning with wishes rather than solutions.

Instead of asking:

> "What should we build?"

We ask:

> **"What wish are we trying to grant?"**

Understanding the wish allows architecture to emerge naturally through discovery.

# Active Wishes

---

## 💭 EKPP-W001

### Wish

> **I wish I could see the initial output of EKPP.**

---

### Why

The project currently needs a tangible, working platform that can be experienced by real readers.

Rather than designing every possible capability up front, EKPP should first demonstrate its core purpose:

Publishing engineering knowledge.

---

### Success Looks Like

A reader can:

* open the EKPP website,
* navigate between EDS, EKS and AFK,
* read the published documentation,
* experience the platform,
* provide meaningful feedback.

---

### Current Status

🟢 Active

---

### Notes

This is intentionally the only active wish.

Granting this wish will generate operational experience that informs future wishes.

# Relationship to Architecture

The Wish List is intentionally maintained before architecture.

The expected flow is:

```text
Wishes

↓

Understanding

↓

Discovery

↓

Architecture

↓

Implementation

↓

Operational Feedback

↓

New Wishes
```

Architecture should emerge from validated wishes rather than anticipated features.

# Current Decision

EKPP will grant one meaningful wish before expanding the platform.

Additional wishes will become active only after operational experience provides evidence that they are needed.
