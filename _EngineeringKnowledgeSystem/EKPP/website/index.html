About EKPP

The Engineering Knowledge Publishing Portal (EKPP) publishes engineering knowledge maintained by the Engineering Knowledge System.

EKPP does not become the source of knowledge. Instead, it transforms living engineering documentation into a publishable reading experience while preserving a single source of truth.

Published Knowledge

  • Assisted Flow of Knowledge (AFK)
  • Engineering Knowledge System (EKS)
  • Engineering Documentation System (EDS)
  • Engineering Knowledge Publishing Portal (EKPP)
“Knowledge should be comfortable to read anywhere.”

Whether someone is working at their desk, waiting for a ride, travelling, or simply enjoying a quiet coffee, engineering knowledge should remain welcoming and easy to consume.

Built through observation. Improved through collaboration.
